About Me

Hey, this is Disip. I belong to Indore, India, a beautiful place in the heart of the country where the food is as good as it can get. I am sure my close Jaipur mates would chuckle and disagree. Alas, Nevermind! I am currently in West Lafayette, US undertaking my doctoral research in multitarget tracking in cislunar space.

We are at the dawn of a new industrial revolution where intelligence and autonomy is taking a center stage. While I am immensely grateful to be witnessing such an exciting era, I do feel sad about being the middle children of history — "hundred years too late to explore Earth and hundred years too early to explore Space". So I am trying to make the best of it by trying to further our species' strides in space.

My current work focuses on developing active sensor tasking algorithms for target reacquisition in cislunar space. In my previous work, I formulated the sensor management problem as a Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP) and planned sensor motion to maximize information gain using Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS).
